# Card Terms and Conditions

Synthetic document written for this coursework. It is not the terms of any real bank. Version 2026-07-01.

## 1. Scope

1.1 These terms apply to every debit and credit card the Bank issues to a customer, physical or virtual.

1.2 The card stays the property of the Bank. The customer may use it only for lawful payments and cash withdrawals.

## 2. Using the card

2.1 The card can be used at shops with a card terminal (chip, contactless or magnetic stripe), at ATMs, and online.

2.2 Online payments require 3-D Secure authentication. The customer confirms each online payment with a one-time password (OTP) sent by SMS or with the Bank's mobile app.

2.3 Contactless payments without a PIN are accepted up to 1,000,000 VND per payment. Larger contactless payments ask for the PIN.

2.4 Payments in a foreign currency are converted to VND at the card network's exchange rate on the day the payment is settled. A foreign transaction fee applies (see the fee schedule).

## 3. Declined payments

3.1 The Bank may decline a payment when the card is locked or expired, when the balance or credit limit is not enough, when the PIN or OTP is wrong, or when the payment looks unusual for the card.

3.2 To protect the customer, the Bank checks every payment automatically before approving it. A payment that looks unusual may be declined or may require an extra confirmation (OTP or biometric check in the app). No money is taken for a declined payment.

3.3 The Bank does not share the details of its fraud checks. Customer service can explain whether a decline was caused by the balance, the card status, the authentication, or a security check.

3.4 If the customer made the declined payment, they can try again after confirming it in the app, or contact customer service.

## 4. Lost cards and unauthorized payments

4.1 The customer must keep the card, the PIN and the OTP secret, and must lock the card in the app or call the hotline as soon as the card is lost or stolen, or the customer suspects that someone else knows the card details.

4.2 The customer is not liable for unauthorized payments made after the card was locked or reported. For unauthorized payments made before that, the customer is not liable when they report them within 60 days after the payment date, unless the Bank shows that the customer acted with gross negligence (for example, writing the PIN on the card or sharing an OTP).

4.3 A payment confirmed with the customer's own OTP or biometric check is treated as authorized, unless the customer shows that the OTP or device was used without their consent.

4.4 A locked card can be unlocked in the app at any time if the customer finds it. A card reported stolen cannot be unlocked and is replaced.

## 5. Card limits

5.1 Each card has a daily spending limit and a daily cash withdrawal limit, shown in the app. The customer may lower the limits in the app at any time, and may ask the Bank to raise them.

5.2 Online payments and payments abroad can be turned off and on separately in the app.

## 6. Contact

6.1 The 24/7 hotline and the in-app chat handle lost cards, declined payments and disputes in Vietnamese and English.
