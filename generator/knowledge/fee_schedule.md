# Card Fee Schedule

Synthetic document written for this coursework. It is not the fee schedule of any real bank. Version 2026-07-01. Amounts include VAT.

## 1. Issuing and replacing cards

| Item | Debit card | Credit card |
|---|---|---|
| Issuing a first card | free | free |
| Annual fee | free | 300,000 VND (waived when spending is at least 50,000,000 VND per year) |
| Replacing a lost or damaged card | 50,000 VND | 100,000 VND |
| Replacing a card locked because of a fraud dispute | free | free |
| Express card delivery (2 working days) | 30,000 VND | 30,000 VND |

## 2. Payments and cash

| Item | Debit card | Credit card |
|---|---|---|
| Payments in VND in Vietnam | free | free |
| Foreign transaction fee (payments in a foreign currency, or to a merchant abroad) | 2.5% of the amount | 3.0% of the amount |
| Cash withdrawal at the Bank's ATMs | free | 4% of the amount, minimum 60,000 VND |
| Cash withdrawal at other banks' ATMs in Vietnam | 3,300 VND | 4% of the amount, minimum 60,000 VND |
| Cash withdrawal abroad | 4% of the amount, minimum 60,000 VND | 4% of the amount, minimum 60,000 VND |

## 3. Credit card charges

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Interest on unpaid balance | 28% per year, charged when the statement balance is not paid in full by the due date |
| Late payment fee | 5% of the minimum payment, minimum 100,000 VND |
| Over-limit fee | 4% of the amount over the limit |

## 4. Disputes

4.1 Opening a dispute is free. If a dispute about a payment the customer actually made is rejected after investigation, a handling fee of 80,000 VND applies.
