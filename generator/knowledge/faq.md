# Card Support FAQ

Synthetic document written for this coursework. It is not the FAQ of any real bank. Version 2026-07-01.

## Why was my card declined?

The most common reasons are: not enough balance or credit limit, a wrong PIN or OTP, a locked or expired card, online or foreign payments turned off in the app, or a security check because the payment looked unusual for your card. No money is taken for a declined payment. Open Card > Transactions in the app to see the payment and the reason category.

## My payment was declined but I made it. What should I do?

Confirm the payment in the app when asked, then try again. If the payment is abroad or online, check that foreign and online payments are turned on in the app. If it is declined again, contact customer service.

## I see a payment I did not make. What should I do?

Lock your card in the app immediately (Card > Lock card), then report the payment as not made by you (Card > Transactions > select the payment > Report a problem). You are not liable for unauthorized payments reported within 60 days of the payment date (Card terms 4.2).

## Will I get my money back for a fraudulent payment?

For a reported unauthorized payment, the Bank gives a provisional refund within 5 working days while it investigates, and most cases are closed within 45 days (Dispute policy 4.2 and 4.4).

## I am travelling abroad. Do I need to tell the Bank?

You do not need to call, but turn on foreign payments in the app before you travel. Payments abroad have a foreign transaction fee of 2.5% for debit cards and 3.0% for credit cards (Fee schedule 2).

## I did not receive the OTP.

Check that your phone number in the app is correct and your phone can receive SMS, then use the in-app confirmation instead. The Bank never asks for your OTP by phone or message (Authentication rules 4.1).

## Why does the app ask for a face scan for a transfer?

Under Decision 2345/QD-NHNN, a face scan is required for a transfer of 10,000,000 VND or more, when your transfers in one day go above 20,000,000 VND, and for the first transfer on a new device (Authentication rules 2.1 to 2.3).

## How do I unlock my card?

If you locked the card yourself and found it, unlock it in the app (Card > Unlock card). A card reported stolen or locked because of a fraud dispute cannot be unlocked; the Bank sends a free replacement (Card terms 4.4, Dispute policy 5.1).

## How long does a dispute take?

The Bank confirms the dispute within 1 working day. Most disputes are closed within 45 days; cross-border disputes can take up to 90 days. You can follow the status in the app (Dispute policy 4).

## Is there a limit for contactless payments?

Contactless payments without a PIN are accepted up to 1,000,000 VND per payment; above that the terminal asks for your PIN (Card terms 2.3).
