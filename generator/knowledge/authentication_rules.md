# Authentication Rules for Payments and Transfers

Synthetic document written for this coursework. It is not the rules of any real bank. Version 2026-07-01. It follows the State Bank of Vietnam's Decision 2345/QD-NHNN, applied by the Bank from 2024-07-01.

## 1. Card payments

1.1 Online card payments are confirmed with 3-D Secure: a one-time password (OTP) by SMS, or a confirmation in the Bank's app.

1.2 Payments at shop terminals are confirmed with the chip and PIN, or are contactless (PIN required above 1,000,000 VND).

1.3 ATM withdrawals always require the PIN.

1.4 The Bank may ask for an extra confirmation in the app for a card payment that looks unusual, for example a first payment from a new device or a payment far from the customer's usual locations.

## 2. Transfers in the mobile app

2.1 A biometric check (face scan matched with the chip of the customer's citizen ID card) is required for a single transfer of 10,000,000 VND or more.

2.2 A biometric check is also required when the total of the customer's transfers in one day goes above 20,000,000 VND.

2.3 A biometric check is required for the first transfer after logging in on a new device.

2.4 Smaller transfers are confirmed with the app PIN or the device's own biometric unlock (Smart OTP).

## 3. New devices

3.1 Logging in on a new device sends a notice to the customer's registered phone number and email.

3.2 The customer can see the devices logged in to their account in the app and remove any device they do not recognize.

## 4. OTP safety

4.1 The Bank never asks for an OTP, a PIN or a password by phone, SMS, email or social media. Anyone asking for an OTP is not the Bank.

4.2 An OTP is valid for 3 minutes and for one payment only. After 5 wrong OTPs the card's online payments are paused for 30 minutes.

4.3 If the OTP does not arrive, check that the phone number in the app is correct and that the phone can receive SMS; the in-app confirmation can be used instead.
