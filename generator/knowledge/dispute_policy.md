# Dispute and Chargeback Policy

Synthetic document written for this coursework. It is not the policy of any real bank. Version 2026-07-01.

## 1. What a dispute is

1.1 A dispute is a request from the customer to reverse a card payment that they did not make, that was charged twice or with the wrong amount, or for goods or services that never arrived.

1.2 Disputes about unauthorized payments (fraud) are handled first. The card is locked when the dispute is opened, so no further payments can be made with it.

## 2. How to open a dispute

2.1 Open a dispute in the app (Card > Transactions > select the payment > Report a problem) or through customer service.

2.2 Each dispute covers one payment. For several unknown payments, report each of them; customer service can open them together.

2.3 The customer is asked for the reason (not made by me, duplicate charge, wrong amount, not received) and, for goods not received, the order details and any messages with the merchant.

## 3. Deadlines

3.1 Report an unauthorized payment within 60 days after the payment date. Reports after 60 days are reviewed case by case, and the customer may be liable for the amount.

3.2 Report a duplicate charge, a wrong amount or goods not received within 60 days after the payment date, and after first contacting the merchant.

## 4. What happens next

4.1 The Bank confirms the dispute in the app within 1 working day and gives it a reference number.

4.2 For unauthorized payments, the Bank refunds the amount as a provisional credit within 5 working days while it investigates, unless the payment was confirmed with the customer's own OTP or biometric check (see Card terms 4.3).

4.3 The Bank asks the card network (Visa, Mastercard or Napas) for a chargeback from the merchant's bank. Card networks allow up to 45 days for the merchant to respond.

4.4 Most disputes are closed within 45 days; complex cross-border disputes may take up to 90 days. The customer can follow the status in the app.

4.5 If the dispute is accepted, the provisional credit becomes final. If it is rejected, the Bank explains why and the provisional credit is reversed with 5 working days' notice.

## 5. Card replacement

5.1 A card locked because of a fraud dispute is replaced free of charge. The new card has a new number; recurring payments must be updated by the customer.
